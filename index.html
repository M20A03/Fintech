Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you get started.

What is Akoin Fintech and what do you offer?

Akoin Fintech Private Limited is a Gurgaon-based financial technology company providing payment gateways, payment links, recurring billing, business banking, payouts, lending, risk analytics, and blockchain solutions for businesses of all sizes across India.

How quickly can I start accepting payments?

Most businesses can go live within hours. Our KYC process is entirely digital — just provide your PAN, GSTIN, and bank account details. Once verified, start accepting payments via UPI, cards, net banking, wallets, and more immediately.

Is Akoin Fintech RBI-compliant and secure?

We are fully compliant with RBI guidelines, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified, and follow SOC 2 Type II practices. All data is encrypted with 256-bit AES encryption both in transit and at rest with regular third-party audits.

What payment modes do you support?

We support 100+ payment modes including all UPI apps, Visa/Mastercard/RuPay debit & credit cards, net banking from 50+ banks, mobile wallets, Buy Now Pay Later, EMI, and international cards. We also support UPI AutoPay for recurring payments.

What are your settlement timelines?

Standard: T+2. Growth plan: T+1. Enterprise: instant T+0 settlements — funds within minutes. No manual intervention required.

Do you provide developer documentation and SDKs?

Yes! REST API documentation, SDKs in Node.js, Python, Java, PHP, and Go, Postman collections, sandbox environments, webhook support with signature verification. Average integration time: under 1 hour.

Can I use Akoin for international payments?

Yes. Our Enterprise plan supports payments from 100+ countries with multi-currency processing, dynamic currency conversion, and INR settlements. Ideal for exporters, SaaS companies, and global freelancers.
