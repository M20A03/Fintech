# IDR Logo — Design Rationale

The isometric cube serves as the central mark, chosen for its geometric precision and its triple symbolism: **structure** (governance frameworks), **risk** (the multidimensional nature of digital threats), and **resilience** (a solid, load-bearing form). The bright orange top face draws the eye upward — conveying ambition and forward motion — while the dark left and right faces ground the mark in authority and seriousness, echoing the high-consequence environments IDR operates in.

The colour palette — **orange (#E8651A), near-black (#1A1A1A) and white** — balances energy with professionalism: orange signals urgency and innovation (fitting for risk and AI), while the monochrome contrast ensures clean reproduction at any size, from favicon to event banner.

Typography uses **Inter** (semi-bold for the "IDR" abbreviation, light-weight for the full name), a contemporary, highly legible sans-serif that reads clearly at small sizes and reinforces IDR's tech/education positioning. The subtle grid lines inscribed on the cube's top face nod to risk matrices and structured assessment, tying the visual identity back to IDR's core discipline.
